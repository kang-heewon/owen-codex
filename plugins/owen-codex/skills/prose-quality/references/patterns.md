# Contextual Prose Patterns

These patterns are diagnostic signals, not a blacklist. A single occurrence is not a defect. Edit only when the pattern obscures meaning, repeats mechanically, or mismatches the destination.

## Signals to Inspect

- **Empty framing:** Announces importance, comprehensiveness, or the response structure before providing useful content.
- **Repeated restatement:** Restates the prompt, repeats conclusions after each section, or duplicates a sentence in bullets.
- **Formulaic contrast:** Repeated `not just A, but B` or `it is not X; it is Y` that adds drama rather than precision.
- **Generic bridges:** Clusters of `Moreover`, `Ultimately`, `나아가`, `궁극적으로`, or `이를 통해` without a real relationship.
- **Unneeded executive-summary voice:** Strategy-memo framing or managerial abstractions where a direct explanation would serve the reader.
- **False agency or vague actors:** Claims that a system or approach “ensures” an outcome without the mechanism or responsible actor.
- **Abstract noun stacking:** Chains such as “implementation of the optimization” where a direct verb is clearer.
- **Nominalized action:** Phrases such as `검토를 수행한다`, `수정을 진행한다`, or `적용을 실시한다` when `검토한다`, `수정한다`, or `적용한다` preserves the meaning.
- **Decorative structure:** Headings for tiny fragments, artificial three-part layouts, repeated label/explanation blocks, or bullets that are harder to read than a paragraph.

## Minimum Effective Edit

Keep each useful idea where it does the most work. Remove empty framing and duplication; replace abstractions only when the source supports a more direct statement; retain orientation, contrast, transitions, repetition, and structure when they carry real meaning.

Do not let cleanup create sterile prose. Useful nuance, personality, humor, transitions, deliberate rhythm, and occasional long sentences may remain. Do not make every sentence short, every paragraph symmetrical, or every statement telegraphic. Stop when remaining edits express preference rather than correct a supported defect.
